Fernhollow: Garden Sort
A cosy shelf-sort puzzle game

Help and support

Help Pemberton, a short-sighted mole, bring his late Nana's garden centre back to life. Match three of a kind on the shelves before the customers run out of patience, then spend your Sprouts and coins restoring the Shop Floor, the Greenhouse and the Pond.

Questions and problems

Stuck, found a bug, or just want to say hello? Email fernhollowgame@gmail.com. Telling us your phone model and the level number helps.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing break ads. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Optional 🎬 reward videos stay, because you only see them if you choose to.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress? Your progress is saved on your phone and comes across when you restore your phone from a backup.

How do I change my ad choices? In the game, open Settings → Privacy choices. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Privacy

No accounts, and your progress stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
